# Bloom Birth Support

An editable single-page doula template transcribed from the approved `Notes/.../Bloom/Bloom.html` delivery.

The supplied images are mapped as follows: the client-requested belly crop is the organic hero portrait, the newborn hand photograph supports the journey section, and the practitioner portrait appears in About. The uncropped `first photo.jpg` remains source material only because the supplied crop instruction and dedicated cropped file establish the approved hero framing.

Before a customer release, replace the placeholder business name, location, email, experience, qualifications and testimonial attribution through Site Settings and the block inspector. Confirm the customer has permission to publish all personal photography and testimonial details.

Acceptance requires a fresh Delivery import, Gutenberg save/reload, and visual checks at 375px, 820px and 1440px.
